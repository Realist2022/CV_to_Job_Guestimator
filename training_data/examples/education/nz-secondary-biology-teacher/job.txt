Job Title: Senior Secondary Science Teacher (Biology Specialist)
Location: Wellington, New Zealand
Contract Type: Permanent, Full-Time

About the School: We are a progressive, high-achieving state secondary school located in the heart of Wellington. We pride ourselves on a holistic approach to education, fostering not only academic excellence but also the emotional and social well-being of our students. Our Science Department is highly collaborative, well-resourced, and passionate about inquiry-based learning.

About the Role: We are seeking an enthusiastic and experienced Senior Science Teacher to join our faculty starting in Term 1. The successful candidate will primarily be responsible for teaching senior Biology up to NCEA Level 3 and Scholarship, alongside junior general science classes. You will also take on a pastoral care role as a Form Teacher.

Key Responsibilities:
- Plan, deliver, and assess engaging science curriculum for Years 9 through 13.
- Lead the NCEA Level 3 Biology moderation and curriculum design.
- Integrate digital technologies and modern pedagogical practices into daily teaching.
- Maintain a positive, inclusive, and highly managed classroom environment.
- Serve as a Form Teacher, providing pastoral care and academic mentoring.

Required Qualifications & Experience:
- A registered teacher with a current New Zealand Practising Certificate.
- A minimum of 5 years of teaching experience in a New Zealand secondary school environment.
- Must have at least 3 years of direct experience teaching NCEA Level 3 Biology with a track record of high student achievement.
- Require 4+ years of experience in classroom management and pastoral care roles.
- A strong foundation in digital literacy, specifically requiring 2 years of experience using Google Workspace for Education (Google Classroom, Docs, Sheets) in a blended learning environment.
- Proven ability to mentor junior staff or student teachers is highly desirable.
